LFG Collectibles Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

About this application

LFG Collectibles Financial Analytics is an internal application operated by LFG Collectibles to manage eBay business reporting.

Information we process

With account authorization, the application may access eBay sales, orders, fees, payouts, account identifiers, and buyer information included in transaction records.

How information is used

Information is used for accounting, inventory management, financial reporting, and compliance with applicable obligations. Personal information is not sold or used for advertising.

Storage and sharing

Information may be processed using service providers supporting the application's hosting and automation. Access is restricted to authorized business operations.

Retention and deletion

Information is retained only as necessary for legitimate business and legal obligations. Requests to access or delete personal information will be reviewed and handled in accordance with applicable law and eBay requirements.
